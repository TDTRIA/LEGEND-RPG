On the workbench

The world is still being made.

LEGEND is an independent browser RPG in active development. Ashmere and the first Old Road journey establish the current playable foundation. Greyfen's regional activities are growing, while the broader world and its systems continue to be built and tested.

The Dispatch shares meaningful progress without presenting unfinished regions or planned features as complete.
