LEGEND: The Awakening · v0.30.5

The v0.30.5 build contains the mandatory three-part opening annals, dead-lantern road opening, Captain Oric and the Blackroot Wolf tutorial, meaningful traveler choices, turn-based road encounters, and the five-stage Old Road expedition. Phaser 4.1 is now active in production for the persistent world scene, environment atmosphere, transitions, and Ashmere landmarks; most gameplay screens and systems still use the existing browser modules while migration continues.

The road tracks route choices, discoveries, camp reports, time, and Road Pressure. Two travel stretches bring an encounter even on a careful route. Completed five-stage trips have a 65% Road Token roll; early returns do not receive the completion token. The illustrated return ledger shows gold, XP, rare-token outcome, and the field report.

What Is Playable Now

  • A road-born opening
    Three mandatory annals, an unnamed voice, Oric, and the first wolf encounter.
  • Ashmere hub
    Town services, work board, trading, crafting, equipment, people, records, and preparation.
  • Old Road expeditions
    Five stages, two route branches, landmarks, camps, danger, tactical encounters, and a saved journal.
  • Traveler progression
    XP, earned levels, saved attribute allocation, gear, inventory, and expedition rewards.
  • Greyfen frontier content
    Marsh Path activity, Ferry Nets contract, local progression pieces, and the optional Silted Storehouse detour.
  • Desktop-first browser play
    The live HTML5 game is developed for desktop and includes responsive mobile compatibility.

Audio & Engine Status

The 48-asset original sound library and the roughly 10½-minute replacement main-theme MP3 are now merged into production and included in the successful GitHub Pages deployment. The shared audio director keeps one continuous theme loop, switches location ambience with Phaser scene/surface changes, preserves the existing sound toggle, and supplies the new title/menu and battle effects. The remaining audio work is human listening for playback, levels, loop seam, and long-session comfort, plus wiring the remaining one-shot cues to their matching game actions.

The production Phaser migration is still underway. The goal is to move every existing game screen and interactive surface into Phaser—not to rewrite the underlying game systems. Existing controllers, combat and expedition rules, progression, rewards, and saves remain authoritative while each presentation surface is migrated and tested.

Creative Direction

LEGEND's primary creative reference is now Darkest Dungeon. The direction is expedition-led dark fantasy: illustrated scenes, strong character silhouettes, expressive dialogue, tactical encounters whose intent is readable, and field reports that record what happened. We are using these qualities as inspiration only; LEGEND keeps its own world, characters, canon, mechanics, art, and identity.

This is a presentation and art-direction decision, not a pause or replacement for the production Phaser migration. The goal remains to move every existing game screen and interactive surface into Phaser while keeping the existing gameplay controllers, rules, progression, and saves authoritative.

Project Status

Ashmere & the Old Road

The opening-to-first-expedition loop is in the production build. More balance and real-player feedback are still needed.

Greyfen

Several local systems are playable, but the complete story, services, and economy acceptance pass remains in progress.

Ironpass

Future region. It is not represented as a complete playable town yet.

The first three towns remain a development target. LEGEND is actively in development; this page does not claim a finished 1.0 release.

What Comes Next

The top technical priority is finishing the full production Phaser migration: persistent HUD/navigation/drawers, Ashmere and services, Old Road and its supporting screens, combat, results and level-up, dungeons/regions, inventory, settings/account, and remaining overlays. Every screen must use the existing production controllers and preserve current saves, rules, rewards, and account behavior. Then complete the deployed soundtrack listening pass and remaining event hooks, and finish Greyfen as a coherent town-and-expedition loop. Balance, accessibility, and desktop/mobile regression checks remain release requirements.
