Greyfen Is Taking Shape — But It Is Not Finished Yet

Greyfen is meant to feel different from Ashmere: a working lowland settlement built around water, raised walks, salvage, ferry traffic, and practical decisions about unsafe crossings. The current build contains several steps toward that identity, but the larger region still has work ahead.

What is in the build

  • Patch the Ferry Nets: a Greyfen-specific Work Board contract that ties local work to the town's fishing and ferry life.
  • Marsh Path: a regional expedition thread with route choices, field evidence, and crossing-related consequences.
  • Silted Storehouse: an optional salvage detour with a disclosed one-hour and +6 Road Pressure cost, one Dry Peat Brick, saved map/journal progress, and a return to Mossbarrow Shelter. It does not block the Waterline Gauge objective.
  • Local progression: the build includes Greyfen-specific resource, crafting, and equipment pieces that connect salvage to later preparation.

The story needs to stay human

The crossing investigation is grounded in a human decision made under trade pressure. The Landing Warden reopened the lower crossing early, without signing the order because she intended to inspect it again. She is a sympathetic wrong-doer, not a supernatural villain. The player should be able to understand the pressure behind the decision and choose how to respond.

The missing closure order and related evidence remain part of the investigation. The game should not invent a culprit or prematurely explain the deeper mysteries of the world just to close the quest quickly.

What remains unfinished

Greyfen still needs a full service-and-story acceptance pass: its landing, inn, market, forge/workbench, people, Boundary Archive, Work Board, Marsh Path, local economy, quest resolution, and region-specific rewards must work together as one complete loop. The Ironpass destination is not being presented as a complete town. The first three towns remain the larger release target, not a claim about current completion.

The next region should open only when the player has a real reason to go there, a coherent activity to do, and a meaningful reward to bring home.
