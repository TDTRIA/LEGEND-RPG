The First Journey: A Road That Can Bite Back

The Old Road is no longer just a sequence of random checks. It is the first real journey in LEGEND: a route with landmarks, choices, danger, discoveries, and a reason to return to Ashmere.

The story starts before the town

New travelers first pass through three mandatory annals that establish the old war, the ancient seal, and the danger surrounding the realm. The opening then moves to the Old Road, beneath a dead lantern. The voice remains unnamed as ???. Captain Oric is tired, wary, and practical; the Blackroot Wolf encounter teaches the player to use the existing ACT command to awaken and fight back.

Traveler creation is framed as recovering a self through memory: identity, personality, origin, calling, and keepsake. Those choices are more than decoration; starting bonuses and carried items continue through the existing player record.

A five-stage expedition with real risk

The Old Road has five expedition stages, two route branches, distinct local scenes, recorded discoveries, field notes, camp reports, a clock, and Road Pressure. The High Ridge and Reed Cut offer different information and risks, not a promise that one route is completely safe.

  • Two travel stretches force a road encounter, even when careful choices keep Pressure low.
  • Higher Road Pressure makes threats more dangerous; preparation improves the odds but never removes uncertainty.
  • Landmarks and interactions update the saved map and field journal.
  • Waystation choices can spend supplies, adjust Pressure, or add a report to the journey.

The goal is to make the player choose how to travel, then live with what the road brings back.

Rewards should matter — and rare rewards should stay rare

At the current baseline of a completed five-stage run at 20 Road Pressure, settlement is 80 gold and 33 XP. The completion reward includes a 65% Road Token roll; it is deliberately not guaranteed. Early returns do not receive the completion token. Rare Bandit and Road Cache drops remain separate possibilities.

Bell Fragments are also uncommon: the Reed Cut chance is 35%, recurring clue interactions are 25%, and a Lantern Wisp drop is 15%. The game records whether a fragment was actually recovered so the return report and town dialogue do not claim evidence the traveler never found.

The return screen is an illustrated field ledger with route, journey progress, discoveries, gold, XP, token outcome, and level progress. When a level is earned, the existing saved attribute-allocation screen lets the player decide how the traveler grows.

What this does not mean

This is a production slice, not a finished game. Ashmere and the first Old Road loop are the main playable foundation. Greyfen has several connected activities but is still under development; Ironpass and the full first-three-town release are not complete. Long-session balance, more human playtesting, and further regional content remain work to do.

The production runtime and existing save key remain in place. This release does not require a new game, a save reset, or an engine migration.
