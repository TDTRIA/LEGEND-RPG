Ashmere and the Old Road Rework

LEGEND is moving into its next phase. The recovery work is no longer the identity of the project. The goal now is to make the game feel like a complete RPG loop that works naturally on a phone.

The problem

Ashmere had grown into a collection of useful screens, but it was too easy to lose the sense of where the player was supposed to go next. The Old Road had the opposite problem: it could feel like repeated random checks rather than an expedition with a beginning, middle, and end.

That made the first loop feel more like a prototype than a game.

Ashmere becomes the base camp

The town is now being organized around five destinations:

  • Inn — recover, prepare, and hear rumors.
  • Market — buy supplies and sell what the road brings home.
  • Forge & Workshop — improve equipment and craft useful items.
  • Town Hall — people, jobs, records, and progression.
  • Old Road Gate — start the next expedition.

The important part is not the number five. It is that every destination now has a recognizable reason to exist in the progression loop.

The Old Road becomes an expedition

The new road layer uses five stages and a Road Pressure meter. Instead of asking the player to repeatedly pick another random route, each choice advances the expedition.

  • Read the Road lowers pressure and favors safer resolution.
  • Search the Margins looks for resources and discoveries.
  • Push Deeper raises pressure and increases the chance of danger.

Combat happens inside that expedition. Falling back is always an available choice, and reaching the final stage gives the player a clear reason to secure the run and return home.

What happens to the D20?

The skill-check idea is not being thrown away. The visible experience is changing.

Instead of putting a die, modifier, and DC in front of the player for every decision, the game can use the same underlying character information to resolve a choice quietly. Skills, class, origin, keepsake, gear, pressure, and preparation can influence the result without interrupting the flow every few seconds.

The intention is simple: the player chooses what to do; the character build helps determine how well it goes.

What comes next

This pass is the foundation for the larger progression loop. Next comes deeper traveler progression, gear and mastery, stronger town relationships, more road events and enemies, deeper road tiers, and rewards that consistently feed back into Ashmere.

The long-term goal is a game with the satisfying progression clarity of an idle RPG while keeping LEGEND's own town, encounters, choices, story, and road identity.
