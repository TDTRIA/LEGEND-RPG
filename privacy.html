Privacy Notice

LEGEND: The Awakening · Development Dispatch

This notice explains how the LEGEND Development Dispatch handles email addresses submitted through the newsletter signup form.

What we collect

The signup form requests your email address and records the consent choice you make on the form. It does not ask for a name, game account, password, or payment information.

Why we use it

Your email address is used to send occasional development updates about LEGEND: The Awakening, including playable milestones, gameplay and world-building notes, artwork, audio, and important project announcements. The Dispatch is not sent on a fixed schedule.

Email service provider

The newsletter signup and email delivery are managed through Brevo. When you subscribe, the information you submit is transmitted to Brevo so it can record the subscription, manage confirmation and unsubscribe requests, and deliver the newsletter. Brevo may also process limited information for platform security and fraud prevention. See Brevo's Privacy Policy.

Confirmation and unsubscribe

Signup uses double opt-in. Your address is not added as a confirmed newsletter subscriber until you follow the confirmation link sent to that address. Every newsletter includes an unsubscribe option. You may also request removal or correction through the LEGEND feedback page.

Retention and sharing

Your email address is kept in the newsletter service while you remain subscribed and as needed to honor unsubscribe requests and applicable record-keeping requirements. We do not sell newsletter subscriber information. Subscriber details are not used for unrelated advertising by the LEGEND project.

Contact

For questions about the LEGEND Development Dispatch or a request concerning your subscription, use the feedback page and select the appropriate contact option.

This notice applies to the LEGEND Development Dispatch newsletter signup. It does not describe all data handling inside the playable game or any separate third-party services a player may choose to use.
