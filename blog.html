Where Development Stands

Ashmere & the Old Road

The first-session loop is in the production build: the road-born opening, Oric tutorial, route choices, danger, discoveries, journal, return report, and rewards. Balance and longer human playtesting continue.

Greyfen

Greyfen has a distinct marsh setting and several playable systems, including Ferry Nets work and the optional Silted Storehouse. Its full story, services, and progression loop still need completion and validation.

Ironpass & Beyond

Ironpass is not yet a complete playable town. It remains future content until its hub, expedition, quest, rewards, and return loop are ready.

The first three towns are a development target, not a feature claim: only content that is actually playable is described as shipped.
