Current Focus

First 10 Minutes

Title screen, intro, Ashmere, road travel, inventory, combat, and whether the game feels alive instead of barebones.

10–15 Minute Playtest Goal

You do not need to finish everything. Read the mandatory annals, shape your traveler, meet Oric and face the Blackroot Wolf, then follow the guided first expedition. If you reach Ashmere, tell us whether the journey made you want to keep going.

1

Create a traveler

Read the three mandatory annals, then make the memory-led choices for identity, personality, origin, calling, and keepsake. Tell us whether your traveler feels personal.

2

Watch the intro

Check whether the opening carries you from the annals into the road awakening. Tell us whether the shift into the living game feels clear and atmospheric.

3

Reach Ashmere

Meet Captain Oric and face the Blackroot Wolf. Tell us whether Oric's dialogue and the first turn-based fight teach the game clearly.

4

Travel and fight

Follow Oric onto the guided Old Road expedition. Try the route choices, camp, discoveries, and encounters; then return to Ashmere and explore its services.

What We Need Feedback On

Title Screen Mobile Character Creation Intro Ashmere Old Road Combat Inventory Bugs
  • Does the title screen feel like a real game home screen?
  • Does the new fantasy identity make sense?
  • Did character creation bug out, loop, or feel boring?
  • Did the intro feel immersive or too text-box heavy?
  • Did the objective card tell you what to do next?
  • Did Ashmere feel like a town, or just a menu?
  • Did Old Road travel feel like an adventure?
  • Did combat feel interactive enough?
  • Did inventory and character sheet feel readable?
  • Did anything clip, overlap, vanish, or break on mobile?
  • Would you keep playing after the first 10 minutes?

Brutally honest feedback helps more than praise. If something is boring, confusing, ugly, unfair, inaccessible, or broken, say it.

Current Build Notes

  • LEGEND is an in-development HTML5 browser RPG, desktop-first with responsive mobile compatibility.
  • The opening is road-first: mandatory annals, traveler creation, Oric and the Blackroot Wolf, then the guided Old Road journey.
  • Ashmere is the main home hub. Greyfen has playable activities but its full town, services, story, and economy loop are still being completed.
  • Phaser 4.1 is active in production; the full migration of existing game screens and interactive surfaces remains underway. The existing gameplay controllers, rules, progression, and saves remain authoritative.
  • Your player save is stored in this browser. Save Vault can export/import a portable copy; it is not a cloud-save service.
  • Report anything that overlaps, disappears, shows duplicate buttons behind a panel, fails to save, or becomes unreadable on desktop or mobile.
  • Balance and progression are still being tested. Tell us where rewards feel too generous, too stingy, or where the road feels unfair.
