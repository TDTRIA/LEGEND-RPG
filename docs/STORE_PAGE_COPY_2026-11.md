## Short description

Wake beneath a dead lantern on a road that should not be safe. Shape a traveler from scattered memories, follow Captain Oric into the wilds, and bring home the discoveries, wounds, and rewards of each expedition in this dark-fantasy browser RPG.

## Long description

The road to Ashmere is not a safe one.

In **LEGEND: The Awakening**, you begin beneath a dead lantern with a name, fragments of memory, and more questions than answers. An unnamed voice guides the first steps. Captain Oric finds you on the road, but a Blackroot Wolf attack quickly proves that surviving the journey will take more than luck.

Shape your traveler through identity, personality, origin, calling, and keepsake choices. Then prepare in Ashmere, choose a route across the Old Road, investigate landmarks, manage supplies and Road Pressure, and face turn-based encounters whose enemy intent gives you a chance to read the danger before it strikes.

Every expedition leaves a record. Discoveries and camp reports are saved to your field journal. Return to town with gold, experience, gear, and evidence that can move the local story forward. A Road Token is a rare completion reward, not a guaranteed payout.

### Current playable features

- A story-led opening with three mandatory world-history annals.
- Memory-based traveler creation with meaningful starting bonuses and keepsakes.
- Captain Oric's first-road tutorial and a production turn-based fight against the Blackroot Wolf.
- Ashmere town hub with people, Work Board activities, trading, equipment, crafting, recovery, and preparation.
- Five-stage Old Road expeditions with High Ridge and Reed Cut routes, named locations, discoveries, camps, map and field journal.
- Road Pressure, travel time, enemy intent, recurring encounters, and a retreat/return choice.
- XP, level-ups, saved attribute allocation, equipment, inventory, and expedition result reports.
- Early Greyfen content including local work, Marsh Path activity, and an optional salvage detour.

### A dangerous road, not an automatic reward loop

The Old Road is designed to carry risk. A careful route can help manage Pressure, but it does not promise a fight-free trip. Two travel stretches bring an encounter. Higher Pressure can make threats stronger. A completed five-stage expedition has a 65% Road Token roll; early returns do not receive that completion token.

### Development status

LEGEND is still in active development. Ashmere and the first Old Road loop form the main playable foundation. Greyfen has a number of connected activities, but its complete town, service, story, and progression loops are still being built and validated. Ironpass is planned future content and is not advertised as a complete playable town. The first three towns are a target for development, not a claim that all three are finished.
