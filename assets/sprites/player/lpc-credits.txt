Player character spritesheet — attribution
============================================

Source: Universal LPC Spritesheet Character Generator
https://liberatedpixelcup.github.io/Universal-LPC-Spritesheet-Character-Generator/

The base character art in lpc-base-sheet.png is from the Liberated Pixel Cup (LPC)
community art pool, licensed under CC-BY-SA 3.0 and/or GPL 3.0.

Per the license, you MUST credit the authors when shipping the game. Add a line
like this to the game's Credits screen:

  Character sprites: Liberated Pixel Cup contributors
  (https://liberatedpixelcup.github.io/Universal-LPC-Spritesheet-Character-Generator/)
  CC-BY-SA 3.0 / GPL 3.0

When you regenerate a dressed character at the generator, use its
"Credits (TXT)" button and replace this file with the generated credits list —
it names the exact authors of every layer you selected.
