HOW TO BEGIN

A quick guide to the current fantasy RPG loop in LEGEND: The Awakening. Create a traveler, make a home in Ashmere, prepare for the Old Road, and bring something back that changes your next decision.

Your First Journey

The opening is a guided story journey. Let it teach you the real game before you settle into town.

1

Read the opening annals

The three chapters establish the old monster war and the danger returning to the realm. They are mandatory and cannot be skipped on a new journey.

2

Shape your traveler

Choose a name, identity, personality, origin, calling, and keepsake. These choices shape your starting traveler; the mysterious presence remains ???.

3

Wake beneath the dead lantern

You begin on the Old Road, not in the Ashmere hub. Meet Captain Oric, learn what he knows, and face the Blackroot Wolf in the real turn-based combat system.

4

Follow Oric onto the Old Road

Choose between High Ridge and Reed Cut, inspect discoveries, manage supplies and Road Pressure, make camp decisions, and prepare for encounters. A careful route is not a danger-free route.

5

Return to Ashmere

After the guided first expedition, return with your field report, loot, gold, XP, and discoveries. Your journal records places and important choices.

6

Prepare for the next trip

Use the Inn, Market, Forge/Workshop, Work Board, people, records, and crafting to recover, take local work, improve gear, and choose what to pursue next.

Current build notes

The current production build is v0.30.4. Ashmere and the Old Road form the main playable foundation. Greyfen has several playable activities, but its complete town, services, story, economy, and progression loop are still being finished. Ironpass is future content. Phaser 4.1 is active as the world-rendering layer, while the wider production migration is still in progress.
